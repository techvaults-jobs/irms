# IRMS: Executive Summary
## One-Page Presentation Brief

---

## 🎯 THE PROBLEM
Requisition approvals take 5-7 days, get stuck in limbo, and nobody knows what's happening. This delays projects, wastes money, and creates frustration.

---

## ✨ THE SOLUTION
**IRMS** is an intelligent approval system that:
- Routes requisitions to the right approvers automatically
- Sends real-time notifications and reminders
- Tracks every decision for compliance
- Gives everyone visibility into spending

---

## 💰 THE IMPACT

|--------|--------|
| **Approval Speed** | 5-7 days → 1-2 days (70% faster) |
| **Monthly Savings** | $11K-22K |
| **Manual Follow-ups** | Eliminated (100%) |
| **Compliance** | 100% audit trail |
| **Employee Satisfaction** | 60% fewer inquiries |

---

## 🚀 HOW IT WORKS (Simple Example)

**Before IRMS:**
1. Sarah submits requisition (Day 1)
2. Sits on manager's desk (Day 2-3)
3. Manager approves, routes to Finance (Day 4)
4. Finance approves (Day 5-6)
5. Sarah finally gets approval (Day 7)

**With IRMS:**
1. Sarah submits requisition (9:00 AM)
2. Manager notified automatically (9:05 AM)
3. Manager approves (9:15 AM)
4. Finance notified automatically (9:20 AM)
5. Finance approves (9:30 AM)
6. Sarah gets approval (9:35 AM) ✅

**Total time: 35 minutes instead of 7 days**
