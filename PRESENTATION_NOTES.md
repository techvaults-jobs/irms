## 🎯 THE PROBLEM WE'RE SOLVING

### Before IRMS: The Pain Points

**Imagine this scenario:**
- Sarah from Marketing needs office supplies. She fills out a paper form.
- The form sits on her manager's desk for 3 days.
- Her manager approves it, but now it needs Finance approval.
- Finance doesn't know it's waiting. Another 2 days pass.
- Finally approved, but nobody knows the actual cost or when it was paid.
- 6 days later, Sarah gets her supplies. The project deadline has passed.

**The Real Cost:**
- ⏱️ **Delays**: Requisitions stuck in approval limbo for days/weeks
- 💰 **Wasted Money**: No visibility into spending. Duplicate orders. Overspending.
- 😤 **Frustration**: Employees don't know status. Managers chase approvals manually.
- 📋 **No Accountability**: Nobody knows who approved what, when, or why
- 🔍 **Compliance Risk**: Can't prove proper approvals happened

---

## ✨ WHAT IS IRMS?

**IRMS is a smart approval and spending management system that:**
- Automates the entire requisition workflow
- Ensures the right people approve at the right time
- Gives everyone real-time visibility
- Tracks every decision for compliance
- Sends automatic reminders so nothing gets forgotten

**Think of it as:** A personal assistant that manages all your purchase approvals, keeps everyone informed, and never lets anything fall through the cracks.

## 🚀 KEY FEATURES & BUSINESS IMPACT

### 1. **Smart Approval Routing**
**What it does:**
- Automatically routes requisitions to the right approvers based on amount and department
- No more guessing who needs to approve
- Approvals happen in parallel when possible (faster) or sequence when needed

**Business Impact:**
- ✅ Approval time reduced from 5-7 days to 1-2 days
- ✅ Eliminates manual routing errors
- ✅ Ensures compliance with company policies

**Real Example:**
- $500 office supply request → Auto-routes to Department Manager only
- $5,000 equipment request → Auto-routes to Manager + Finance + Director
- System knows the rules. No confusion.

## 💡 HOW IT WORKS: A Day in the Life

### **9:00 AM - Sarah (Staff) Submits a Requisition**
- Logs into IRMS
- Fills out form: "Need 10 office chairs, $2,500"
- Uploads quote from vendor
- Clicks "Submit"
- System automatically routes to her manager

### **9:15 AM - Manager Gets Notified**
- Email: "New requisition awaiting your approval"
- Logs in, sees Sarah's request with quote attached
- Reviews: "Looks good, approved"
- Clicks "Approve" with comment: "Approved for office renovation"
- System automatically routes to Finance

### **9:30 AM - Finance Reviews**
- Email: "New requisition awaiting your approval"
- Sees: $2,500 office chairs, manager approved, quote attached
- Checks budget: "We have room in this category"
- Clicks "Approve"
- System marks as "Fully Approved"

### **9:35 AM - Sarah Gets Notified**
- Email: "Your requisition has been approved!"
- Logs in, sees: "Status: APPROVED"
- Can now proceed with ordering
- Total time: 35 minutes (vs. 5-7 days before)

### **Later - Finance Tracks Payment**
- Vendor sends invoice
- Finance logs payment in IRMS
- System records: amount paid, date, reference number
- Audit trail complete
